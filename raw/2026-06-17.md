- 발행: 2026-06
- 본문:
  > On May 14, 2026, Colorado Governor Jared Polis signed into law SB26-189 (the Colorado ADMT Act), repealing and replacing the landmark 2024 Colorado AI Act.
  > The new law imposes targeted obligations on developers and deployers of automated decision-making technology used in consequential decisions (including employment processes), requiring advance notice, post-decision disclosures, and certain consumer rights.
  > Enforcement is scheduled to begin on January 1, 2027. The law centralizes enforcement with the Colorado Attorney General and introduces a fault-based apportionment approach to discrimination liability.
  > The amended law exempts AI-assisted decisions concerning advertising and marketing, summarization and translation tools that present information for human review, and consumer-facing chatbots, subject to an acceptable use policy.
