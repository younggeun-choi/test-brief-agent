## 1. Google Gemini CLI 서비스 종료, Antigravity CLI로 전환

- 출처: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318660/20260618/gemini-cli-shutdown-takes-effect-ci-cd-pipelines-break-go-based-antigravity-cli-arrives.htm
- 발행: 2026-06-18
- 본문:
  Gemini CLI and Gemini Code Assist IDE extensions stopped serving consumer requests on June 18, 2026, as Google ended service for all free, Google AI Pro, and Ultra subscribers.
  Google replaced the open-source coding tool with Antigravity CLI, a closed-source Go binary that drops free-tier requests from 1,000 per day to roughly 20.
  Every CI/CD pipeline, shell script, and IDE integration that calls the gemini command stopped receiving responses on June 18, 2026.
  The Google Antigravity ecosystem consists of a standalone desktop application (Antigravity 2.0) and a high-performance, Go-based terminal interface (Antigravity CLI), both powered by a unified shared agent harness designed for complex, multi-agent orchestration.
  Enterprise access for Gemini CLI and Gemini Code Assist IDE extensions remains unchanged.

- 발행: 2026-06-18 (참조)
- 본문:
  Goldman Sachs estimates roughly $7.6 trillion of cumulative capital expenditure between 2026 and 2031 across AI infrastructure including compute, data centers, and power.
  At $765 billion in 2026 alone, annual AI capex is expected to reach $1.6 trillion by 2031; combined spending from Meta, Microsoft, Amazon and Alphabet is projected at $5.3 trillion by 2030.

  Google is paying SpaceX $920 million per month for compute, while Anthropic is paying SpaceX $1.25 billion per month for the Colossus 1 facility.
  The firm stresses that this figure is highly dependent on supply-side assumptions, including chip depreciation timelines, data center costs, compute architecture choices, and construction cycles.
