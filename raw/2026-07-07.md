## 1. Global push for AI governance amid warnings of 'catastrophic harm'
- 출처: https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167862
- 발행: 2026-07-06
- 본문:
  Governments, tech companies, academics and civil society spent two days at the Global Dialogue on AI Governance wrestling with how to regulate a technology that is evolving faster than the rules meant to contain it. UN Secretary-General António Guterres said AI "could compress decades of development into years" and become "the great equalizer of the 21st century." He added that "when countries align on how to test systems, measure risk and assign responsibility, safety travels with the technology." The two-day Global Dialogue on AI Governance, convened by the UN General Assembly, was attended by delegates from all 193 member states and opened in Geneva on 6 July.

## 2. Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva, 6–7 July
- 출처: https://www.un.org/global-dialogue-ai-governance/en
- 발행: 2026-07-06
- 본문:
  The first session of the Global Dialogue on AI Governance was held on 6 and 7 July 2026 in Geneva, with a second session to follow in New York in May 2027. The AI Dialogue is the United Nations platform where all governments and stakeholders convene to discuss international cooperation, share best practices and lessons learned, and facilitate open, transparent and inclusive discussions on artificial intelligence governance. It marks the moment the international community officially acknowledged that AI is no longer only a technology issue but a geopolitical one.
